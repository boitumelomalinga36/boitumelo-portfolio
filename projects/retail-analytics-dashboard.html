Deliverable 1

Project Charter

Background

A national retail chain operates multiple stores across South Africa and generates thousands of transactions every month. While large amounts of sales and inventory data are collected, management relies on static spreadsheets and manual reports to monitor business performance.

This makes it difficult to identify sales trends, monitor profitability, optimise inventory and make timely business decisions.

Business Problem

Current reporting is fragmented across multiple spreadsheets and departments. Management cannot easily identify high-performing stores, underperforming products, emerging sales trends or inventory issues.

This causes delayed decision making, limited operational visibility, poor inventory planning, manual report preparation and inconsistent KPI reporting.

Project Goal

Design an executive retail analytics dashboard that consolidates retail data into meaningful visualisations and KPIs, enabling management to make faster, data-driven business decisions.

Project Objectives

Provide a single source of truth for retail performance.Track revenue, profit and sales trends.Compare store and regional performance.Identify best-selling and low-performing products.Improve inventory visibility.Support executive and operational decision-making.

SQL Analysis

Sample Queries Used for Insight Generation

These SQL examples show the type of analysis used to validate KPIs and produce dashboard-ready insights.

Revenue by Store

SELECT
  s.store_name,
  s.province,
  SUM(sa.revenue) AS total_revenue
FROM sales sa
JOIN stores s ON sa.store_id = s.store_id
GROUP BY s.store_name, s.province
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;

Monthly Sales Trend

SELECT
  DATE_FORMAT(order_date, '%Y-%m') AS sales_month,
  SUM(revenue) AS total_revenue,
  SUM(profit) AS gross_profit
FROM sales
GROUP BY DATE_FORMAT(order_date, '%Y-%m')
ORDER BY sales_month;

Top 10 Products

SELECT
  p.product_name,
  p.category,
  SUM(sa.quantity) AS units_sold,
  SUM(sa.revenue) AS total_revenue
FROM sales sa
JOIN products p ON sa.product_id = p.product_id
GROUP BY p.product_name, p.category
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
LIMIT 10;

Profit by Category

SELECT
  p.category,
  SUM(sa.revenue) AS total_revenue,
  SUM(sa.profit) AS gross_profit,
  ROUND(SUM(sa.profit) / NULLIF(SUM(sa.revenue), 0) * 100, 2) AS profit_margin
FROM sales sa
JOIN products p ON sa.product_id = p.product_id
GROUP BY p.category
ORDER BY gross_profit DESC;

Low Stock Items

SELECT
  s.store_name,
  p.product_name,
  i.stock_on_hand,
  i.reorder_level
FROM inventory i
JOIN stores s ON i.store_id = s.store_id
JOIN products p ON i.product_id = p.product_id
WHERE i.stock_on_hand <= i.reorder_level
ORDER BY i.stock_on_hand ASC;

Underperforming Stores

SELECT
  s.store_name,
  s.province,
  SUM(sa.revenue) AS total_revenue,
  SUM(sa.profit) AS gross_profit
FROM sales sa
JOIN stores s ON sa.store_id = s.store_id
GROUP BY s.store_name, s.province
HAVING total_revenue < 250000
ORDER BY total_revenue ASC;

Findings

Business Insights

The dashboard converts retail data into clear operational and strategic findings.

Insight 1: Gauteng stores generated the highest revenue.

Impact: The region demonstrates consistently high customer demand.

Recommendation: Increase inventory allocation and marketing investment within Gauteng.

Insight 2: Some products had high sales volume but low profit margins.

Impact: Revenue growth is not translating into profitability.

Recommendation: Review pricing strategy and supplier costs.

Insight 3: Some stores consistently underperformed.

Impact: Operational inefficiencies may exist.

Recommendation: Review staffing levels, local marketing initiatives and stock availability.

Decision Support

Recommendations

Recommendations were aligned to the business problem, insights and dashboard objectives.

Prioritise Gauteng Inventory

Increase inventory allocation to high-performing Gauteng stores where demand is consistently strong.

Review Product Margins

Investigate pricing, supplier costs and discounting for high-volume products with weak margins.

Support Underperforming Stores

Review staffing levels, local marketing, stock availability and store operating patterns.

Standardise KPI Reporting

Use the dashboard as the single source of truth for revenue, profit, sales growth and inventory KPIs.

Monitor Reorder Signals

Use low-stock views to reduce stockouts and improve replenishment planning.

Schedule Monthly Reviews

Use monthly trend reports to track whether actions are improving store and category performance.
